The ImGui JS binding binds nine `ImDrawList` functions on `imgui.drawList`
(`addons/imgui/bind/bind_drawlist.cpp`) but not `PushClipRect` /
`PopClipRect` (nor `PushClipRectFullScreen`). A window owns the bottom
entries of its draw list's clip rectangle stack, nothing in ImGui's state
says which entries are the caller's, and popping one of the window's makes
the window's own `End()` pop an empty stack (a plain assertion in
`ImVector`'s `pop_back()` / `back()`, imgui.h:2390, 2377). So a binding that checked only "the
stack is not empty" would let JavaScript crash the process, and one that
checked nothing would be worse. The design
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-imgui-binding-design.md`, Surface,
"Draw list") defers them to part C; `examples/imgui-app/`'s canvas clips by
drawing into a child window instead.

What part C needs: the binding must track which clip rectangles its caller
pushed, per window and per frame (a count of the caller's pushes on the
current window's draw list, checked by `popClipRect`, and the window's
`end()` refusing or repairing a scope left open). That is the same
tracking of caller-opened scopes that the uncovered precondition 3b (a
closer applied to a scope ImGui opened internally) needs; deciding one
decides the other.

Done when `drawList.pushClipRect` / `popClipRect` are bound with a guard
that refuses a pop of a rectangle the caller did not push (with a refusal
case in `test/ui-imgui-bind-errors.js` and a recorded mutation that makes
ImGui abort without it), and the canvas or a test draws clipped by them; or
when part C's design records that they stay unbound and why.
