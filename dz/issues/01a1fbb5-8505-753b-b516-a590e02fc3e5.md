Everything measured for the Metal backend (Task 16) was measured on one
Apple-silicon machine, an Apple M4 Max. No Intel Mac has built or run it,
and neither has the x86_64 slice under Rosetta.

What is known to differ there, from reading Sokol and from the Mac
measurements:

- Sokol selects managed storage for buffers and textures on Intel, shared
  on Apple silicon. Nothing in the backend's design depends on shared
  storage, but nothing was run against managed. The frame capture copies
  the drawable into a shared-storage `MTLBuffer` of its own, which Intel
  GPUs support, and reads it after the command buffer completes.
- The drawable measured was `MTLPixelFormatBGRA8Unorm`, storage mode
  Managed, on the M4; the capture refuses a drawable that is not BGRA8 and
  traces it once, so a different format there would show as a failed
  capture, not a wrong picture.
- Machines with two GPUs: `MTLCreateSystemDefaultDevice()` is taken before
  AppKit is touched and the presenter uses the device Sokol's environment
  reports. On a one-GPU machine those are the same device. Whether they
  can differ on a dual-GPU Intel Mac was not examined.
- The GPU watchdog and the id-pool arithmetic the maintenance pass relies
  on are Sokol's and Metal's, not the GPU's, and are expected to carry
  over; "expected" is all that can be said.

To close this: build with `HERMES_NODE_ENABLE_UI=ON` on an Intel Mac and
run `check-hermes-node` with `HERMES_NODE_UI_EXPECT_DISPLAY=1`, and record
the totals and any failure here.
