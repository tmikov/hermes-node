`addons/imgui/imgui_renderer.cpp` (`makeStreamBuffer`) creates its vertex and
index buffers with `sg_buffer_usage.dynamic_update = true`, because
`sg_append_buffer` refuses an immutable buffer (`VALIDATE_APPENDBUF_USAGE`)
and appending is how two `render()` calls in one tick share one Sokol frame.

At the vendored Sokol pin, `sokol_gfx.h` documents the flag as deprecated:
"dynamic_update is deprecated and will be replaced with a .write_persistent
flag in one of the next updates". It still works at the pin.

When Sokol is bumped: check whether `dynamic_update` still exists and whether
`sg_append_buffer` still accepts its replacement, and move the addon to it.
`test/ui-imgui-draw.js` (TWOPAIRS, MANYTWICE) fails with a `[sokol-` line if
the appends stop being legal.
