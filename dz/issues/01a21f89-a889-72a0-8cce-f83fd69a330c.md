The ImGui JS binding (`addons/imgui/bind/`, 154 functions and 583
constants) and `examples/imgui-app/` have been run only on Linux: GL
backend, under Xvfb, framebuffer scale 1, in `cmake-build-asan` (Debug,
ASAN) and `cmake-build-noasan` (Release), 2026-11-27 and 2026-11-28. Part A
of the addon was run on one Apple-silicon Mac (Metal, 2x); the binding and
the application were not.

Not yet run on the Mac:

- every `test/imgui-*.js` and `test/ui-imgui-*.js` on Metal, in the Debug
  and the Release build, and the whole suite;
- `test/ui-imgui-app.js`, which opens the application with
  `highDpi: true` and prints the scale it saw (2 is expected there);
- the application's measurement (`test/fixtures/imgui/app-measure.js`) in
  the Mac's Debug and Release builds;
- the hand check the design asks for: a person runs
  `examples/imgui-app/app.js` and looks at it, once on Linux and once on
  the Mac (on Linux only a capture has been looked at).

Done when the Mac round has run all of the above, its results are
recorded in `docs/superpowers/plans/progress-imgui-binding.md`, and
anything it found is fixed or filed; the two hand checks are recorded with
their dates (or recorded as not done).
